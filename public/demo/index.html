The walkthrough

  1. Keep the evidence in view. Inspect previously generated real Clocked proposals, their original source phrases, and the distinction between reviewed and unresolved facts.
  2. Ask what changes coverage. In the fictional example, Cy’s venue-access answer previews four filled roles and three reassignments. Dee’s sound qualification alone still fills three. A preview leaves the saved plan unchanged.
  3. Record the actual answer. Enter a clearly fictional confirmation and reviewer. The saved draft reaches four roles and survives reload.
  4. Share the roster, keep the sources private. The roster CSV omits source messages and internal fact history. A private backup contains both.
  5. Use Clocked to propose, then review. Fill proposes editable roles and explicit requirements. Extract anchors exact phrases; Decide proposes separate prerequisite states. Only reviewed affirmative evidence enables assignments.

Language into evidence. Evidence into a plan.

Clocked Fill, Extract and Decide provide structured proposals. An exact min-cost maximum matching engine computes eligibility, coverage and reassignment consequences.

Unknown, conditional, conflicting and unreviewed facts do not establish eligibility. A model’s confidence is never treated as a probability that a person will reply “yes.”

The public example and manual planning need no account. Anonymous analysis is disabled to protect free credits. Opening this demo or the recorded review makes no Clocked call. Workspaces are private to a browser session. One interval, up to 12 volunteers and eight roles.
